description: "The NOMAD CAMELS Plugin allows users to automatically parse all measurement files (HDF5) recorded with [NOMAD CAMELS](https://fau-lap.github.io/NOMAD-CAMELS/). Parsing creates a NOMAD entry out of every CAMELS measurement file. This makes metadata that users entered or that was automatically recorded by CAMELS searchable in NOMAD (Oasis).\n\nThe CAMELS App allows you to easily filter all your NOMAD CAMELS entries. Quickly find your measurement data by filtering on the following metadata:\n\n- Measurements start time\n- Measurements end time\n- Session\n- Tags\n- Measurement description\n- Comments\n- Protocol description\n- Protocol overview\n- User\n- Sample"
