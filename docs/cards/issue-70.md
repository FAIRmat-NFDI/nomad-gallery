description: "The [`NOMAD CAMELS Plugin`](https://github.com/FAIRmat-NFDI/nomad-camels-plugin) allows users to automatically parse (this creates a NOMAD entry out of every CAMELS measurement file) all measurement (HDF5) files recorded with [NOMAD CAMELS](https://fau-lap.github.io/NOMAD-CAMELS/). This makes metadata that users entered or that was automatically recorded by CAMELS **searchable** in NOMAD (Oasis).\n\nThe CAMELS App allows you to easily filter all your NOMAD CAMELS entries. Quickly find your measurement data by filter by sample, measurement time, etc.\n\nMetadata you can search/filter for:\n- Measurements start time\n- Measurements end time\n- Session\n- Tags\n- Measurement description\n- Comments\n- Protocol description\n- Protocol overview\n- User\n- Sample"
