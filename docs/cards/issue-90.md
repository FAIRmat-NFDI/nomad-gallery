description: "CrystalLLM provides a fast, easy route to generating plausible crystal structures for any reasonable inorganic chemical formula. The model uses autoregressive completion, just like regular LLMs, to go from formula to structure. Trained on millions of examples from the NOMAD database, CrystalLLM can be easily adapted and used in computational materials discovery workflows, either proposing new materials by itself, or proposing structures for promising new compositions. CrystalLLM can also be incorporated with experimental characterisation to allow rapid suggestions of reasonable structures."
